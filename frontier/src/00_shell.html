How to play

How the lab runs

One turn is one week. Nothing moves until you tap End Turn. Tap a labelled object on any floor to open its panel; the elevator moves you between floors.

Each week

  1. Rent compute and hire. Training output grows with compute and staff. Rent PF on the Serving floor or in the Boardroom (Compute tab), buy clusters when cash allows, and hire in the Boardroom (Team tab). The default 20 PF and 10 staff will not reach the first milestone.
  2. Set the sliders. Four sliders split compute and staff across Training, Serving, Safety and Research. They always total 100%. Training raises capability on the skill the run targets. Serving earns revenue. Safety raises safety. Research speeds projects and unlocks higher tiers.
  3. Greenlight projects. Research lists the offers. Each has a cash cost spread over its weeks, a duration, a risk of overrun or failure and a payoff. One runs per project floor, three at a time.
  4. Fund the lab. Investors carry the early years: a round adds cash and dilutes your stake, and missing its milestone takes rounds off the table for a year. Revenue grows with capability and public trust.
  5. End Turn. The week resolves. The memo lists what changed, what is flagged and what is due.

The warning ladder

  • Capability and safetyCoding, Reasoning and Agents each have a capability bar and a safety bar. The gap between them is the risk. Agents weighs most.

  • WarningGap above 10 for two weeks. An internal report. No cost.

  • IncidentGap above 20. A chance each week of an incident that costs cash and public trust.

  • Final incidentGap above 30 for four weeks, or a third incident on one skill within a year. The lab closes.

Winning and losing

  • The winHold the frontier safely for 52 weeks in a row: your average capability at or above the best rival’s, and every skill’s safety within 5 of its capability.

  • Two ways to closeCash reaches zero, or the final incident. Either ends the lab for good.

  • The frontier recordA rival that reaches a capability mark first goes on the record and costs score. The run continues.

  • Nothing carries overEach new lab starts from nothing.
