1. Why this batch

V0.3 gave the run a customer era, and it goes quiet once the book is full: five or six accounts sign, renew themselves, and nothing asks the player to decide anything. The balance probe says the same thing from the other side. customer-first wins 5% of seeds while 19 of 20 are alive past year 4. It survives, but it never gets anywhere. The cause is structural. Serving clients spends the PF that training needs, and nothing flows back toward the frontier.

This batch closes the open item in status.md ("the account era goes quiet once the book is full") and makes a big book a real route to the frontier. It does that with three systems: renewal meetings, client asks and sector swings. Client work also trains the model.

3. Systems

3.1 Client work trains a skill (05b_accounts.js reads, 03_model.js applies)

Each sector maps to one skill. The lead's first pass, balanced by name count over the 30 names:

SkillSectorsNames
codingBanking, Retail, Energy, Telecoms11
reasoningInsurance, Pharma, Legal9
agentsLogistics, Public sector10

Put it in FR.accounts.K.sectorSkill as a plain map so it can be retuned.

  • Field training: each week, every live account adds training to its sector's skill. It equals the gain that served × K.field.rate PF would produce on that skill through the normal training path, with no spillover. First pass: rate = 0.35. It uses the same diminishing returns, the same noise and the same safety drift, so client work widens gaps like any training. The tension stays.
  • If sim.output needs a staff term, use the lab's training staff per training PF so the rate means "a PF of client work is worth 0.35 of a training PF".
  • Expose FR.accounts.fieldPF(s) → { coding, reasoning, agents }. FR.model.gains must include it, so the frontier strip and every forecast show it.
  • Serving readout gains one line: "Client work: +0.4 agents, +0.2 coding a week." The Company overview's model section shows the same figure.
  • Only served PF counts. An underserved contract trains less, which is another reason to serve the book in full.

3.2 Renewal meetings (05b_accounts.js)

The meeting opens at K.dueWarn (8 weeks before ends) and replaces the "contract ends" memo line with a decision. It is state on the account, a.meeting = { opens, answer: null }, answered with FR.cmd.renewAccount(id, choice). The choice can change any time until the last contract week, which resolves it.

ChoiceNeedsResult
Renewmood ≥ K.meet.renewMood (45)Same tier and length, fee repriced to the current year, PF unchanged.
Push up a tiertier < 3, mood ≥ K.meet.upMood (60), the lab meets the next tier's minimums at the last weekMood ≥ 70: next tier (fee, length, PF scaled with scalePF as today). Mood 60–69: 50% the client accepts; otherwise it renews at the same tier and the memo says so. A push up never loses the client.
Let go—Leaves at ends, why: 'let go', no trust cost. The name rests K.cooldown weeks like an expired one. The PF frees for the offer board.
  • If the requirement fails on the last week (mood fell under 45, or the lab lost the tier minimums), the contract ends as 'expired' with a memo line that says why. A failed push up falls back to Renew if Renew still qualifies.
  • Show the odds plainly in the panel: "Push up to tier 2: accepts at mood 70+ (now 64: even odds; a refusal renews at tier 1)."
  • Unanswered by the last week: ask the owner (§7). The build default until answered: the contract lapses as 'expired'. The memo warns 8, 4 and 1 weeks out.
  • Remove the automatic renewal branch. K.mood.renew is retired in favour of K.meet. Keep the 'due' memo line kind for the meeting.

3.3 Client asks (05b_accounts.js)

A live account can put one goal to the lab with a deadline. a.ask = { type, skill, target, from, due, weeks, progress, reward, answered }. It is answered with FR.cmd.answerAsk(id, accept). An unanswered ask counts as accepted, so it can't be dodged by ignoring it.

  • When: no sooner than 13 weeks after go-live. Every 13 weeks each live account without an ask rolls K.ask.chance (0.35). At most K.ask.maxOpen (2) asks open across the book, and no ask in an account's last 12 contract weeks, because the meeting is the decision then.
  • Decline up front (within 2 weeks of the ask arriving): mood −5.
  • Met: mood +10 and a one-off bonus of 4 × feePerWeek, paid that week (in revenue, after the incident factor and the DeepField share like any fee).
  • Missed: mood −15.
TypeMemo text (straight tone)Target / check
cap"Coastline Freight asks for agents capability 38 by Nov 2."Sector skill cap ≥ target by due. Target = current + 3..6 (by tier), due 8–16 weeks. Field training (§3.1) helps here, which is intended.
inStep"Halden Mutual asks that every safety stay within 3 of capability for 8 weeks, starting now."Consecutive weeks with every gap ≤ 3 reach weeks before due (due = weeks + 4).
peak"Brightwell Stores asks for 6 more PF from Dec 7 for 8 weeks."Reserved PF rises by the amount for those weeks. Any unserved week misses the ask (the normal unserved mood penalty still applies).
clean"Province Health Authority asks for no incident before Apr 1."No lab incident until due (13–26 weeks).
cost (cold sector only, §3.4)"Northgate Savings Bank asks for a 15% fee cut for the rest of its term."Accept: fee × 0.85 for the rest of the term, mood +10, no bonus. Decline: mood −10.

Weights: cap 35, inStep 25, peak 20, clean 20. Skip a type the lab can't possibly meet in time (e.g. a cap target above what the forecast reaches at full training). An ask the player can't win is noise.

3.4 Sector swings (05b_accounts.js owns them; 06_market.js only prints the news)

accounts.sectors = { hot: { name, until } | null, cold: { name, until } | null, nextAt }.

  • From the account unlock, roll every 26 weeks. 60%: a sector not already swinging turns hot for 13–26 weeks. 40%: one turns cold for 13–26 weeks. At most one hot and one cold at a time. A sector never flips hot to cold (or back) within 26 weeks of its last swing.
  • Hot: that sector is dealt at 3× weight, and offers dealt during the swing carry fees +25% for their whole term. Live accounts in it gain +1 extra mood a week (top 85 instead of 80 while hot). Through §3.1, a hot sector means more client work on its skill.
  • Cold: no offers from it. Live accounts in it lose 1 mood a week unless the lab is in step (FR.sim.inStep). Their asks are always cost. Their renewal meeting needs +10 mood on every threshold.
  • News line on the turn: "Banking budgets tighten; lenders pause new AI contracts." / "Logistics firms race to automate dispatch; buyers want agents." The memo gets one line and the Serving panel gets a sector strip (hot in board blue, cold in grey, weeks left).

3.5 Serving floor and UI (08_hq_floors.js, 09_ui_panels.js, 09_ui_overview.js)

  • Accounts panel rows: add the sector's skill tag (e.g. "Logistics · agents"). An open ask shows as a second line with the target, the progress ("35 / 38 · 6 weeks left") and Accept / Decline while declinable.
  • Meeting card: an account inside its meeting window shows three 48 px buttons (Renew / Push up / Let go), the current choice highlighted, and one line of odds and consequences. A meeting with no answer puts a badge on the Company dock button and on the Serving floor tap target.
  • Sector strip at the top of the Accounts section. It scrolls inside itself, never sideways on the page.
  • Client wall: a plaque with an open ask or meeting gets a small amber tag. Plaques in a hot sector are lit a shade warmer and in a cold sector a shade dimmer. Reuse the plaque material and textures, with no per-frame allocation.
  • Company overview: the Clients section lists open asks, meetings due and the sector strip. Its Open button rides to the Serving floor as today.
  • Memo: raise K.memoMax to 3. Priority: meeting due / unanswered → ask arrived / met / missed → sector swing → signed / churned / go-live.

4. Turn loop change (02_sim.js)

Step 1, training: add FR.accounts.fieldPF to each skill's training input before gains resolve. It uses last week's served PF, the value known at allocation time, so forecasts stay exact.

Step 3, money: after fees resolve, FR.accounts.step runs in this order: sector swing roll → mood (including the hot/cold drift) → ask checks (met/missed, bonus into this week's revenue) → meetings resolved at ends → churn → offers → new asks. shock() after the ladder also fails any open clean ask.
